# Bio Preview - Final Version

## SALIOU M'BOUP

Saliou M'Boup moves fluidly across musical worlds, specializing in West African drumming and teaching. Continuing his family legacy, his father is a Senegalese griot and master drummer who served as director of music for Ballet Bougarabou. From Venice, California, he brings deep roots and authentic tradition to every performance.

Since 2022, he has worked with STAR Education throughout LAUSD, creating curriculum and launching West African Drum classes focused on early childhood development. He also leads STAR's 16-week Rock and Roll course, which culminates in student performances at venues like The Mint and The Troubadour. Along with other Mafé members, he serves as a featured artist at public and private schools across Los Angeles, teaching social and emotional learning through percussion and rhythm.

## SHANNON TOBIN

Shannon Tobin is a multi-instrumentalist who moves effortlessly between genres. A Los Angeles native with deep roots in jazz composition, he brings rare versatility to The Mafé Ensemble. Whether he's laying down bass lines or blending traditions into something entirely new, his presence shapes every performance.

His credits include the US National Tour with Red Elvises (2023–2025), playing bass and balalaika. Currently completing his Music Industry Studies degree at CSUN, bridging performance and professional artistry.

> "Mafé represents more than the name of the food — it's an experience, a connection, a sharing of love with your brothers and sisters, musically and realistically."

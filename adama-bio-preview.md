# Adama Bio Options

## Option 1 (Focus on lineage + travel)
Adama carries the N'Guewel lineage, a Senegalese tradition of musicians and cultural keepers. From age ten, he traveled across West Africa performing in healing ceremonies, immersed in the spiritual power of the drum. His command of multiple percussion traditions shapes every rhythm he plays.

## Option 2 (Focus on depth of knowledge)
Adama is a master percussionist grounded in the N'Guewel lineage. Since childhood, he has traveled West Africa performing in healing ceremonies and studying multiple drum traditions. That deep spiritual foundation lives in every rhythm he brings to the stage.

## Option 3 (Shortest, punchy)
Adama carries the N'Guewel lineage and deep knowledge of West African percussion. From age ten, he traveled the region performing in healing ceremonies, immersed in the drum's spiritual power. That foundation shapes everything he plays.

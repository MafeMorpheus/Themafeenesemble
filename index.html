About

Rooted in tradition.
Alive in Los Angeles.

The Mafé Ensemble is an artist collective blending traditional West African rhythms with a fresh West Coast vibe. Born in the historic Leimert Park neighborhood, the group creates a living dialogue between ancient traditions and the vibrant, ever evolving sounds of LA.

Their music is a diasporic fusion, grounded in deep cultural roots, alive with contemporary energy, and built for connection. Every performance carries an electric spirit of community and joy, whether on a festival stage or in a neighborhood classroom.

The ensemble's credits speak to both their artistry and reach. Opening the LA City Council Juneteenth Celebration to the 2026 Oscars, where founding members joined the acclaimed "I Lied to You" performance from the film Sinners.

Selected performances include Festival Afrique, Steelcraft Summer Safari, The Miracle Theater, Mar Vista Music Project, Poetry and Passion Project, and Creek Psych Festival.

Beyond the spotlight, Mafé is equally committed to education. Offering classes, workshops and interactive performances, bringing music to life for all generations. Mafé members also serve as featured artists at public and private schools, teaching social and emotional learning through percussion and rhythm.

Educational partners include STAR Education, Montessori Schools, ASI Cultural Center, CSUN Music Therapy, and Mendocino Art Center.

Members

Saliou M'Boup

Saliou M'Boup moves fluidly across musical worlds and instruments, specializing in West African drumming and teaching. Raised in Venice Beach, he carries forward a powerful family legacy. His father is a Senegalese griot and master drummer who served as musical director for Ballet Bougarabou. Saliou brings deep roots and authentic tradition to every performance.

Since 2022, Saliou has been partnering with STAR Education throughout LAUSD, creating curriculum and teaching West African drum classes for K–12. With a focus on early childhood development, he also teaches their 16 week RockSTAR Band sessions, guiding students toward live performances at iconic venues like The Mint and The Troubadour.

Shannon Tobin

Shannon Tobin is a multi-instrumentalist whose jazz roots and genre spanning range make him a cornerstone of The Mafé Ensemble. Native to Los Angeles, whether he is drumming, laying down bass lines or pulling from entirely different traditions, his presence shapes every performance.

His credits include the US National Tour with Red Elvises (2023–2025), where he played bass and balalaika. He is currently completing his Music Industry Studies degree at CSUN, bridging performance and professional artistry.

"Mafé is more than food: it's an experience, a connection, love. A tasty dish shared with your brothers and sisters, musically and realistically."

Haki Saliim Mooney

Haki Saliim Mooney is a Los Angeles native steeped in West African drumming, percussion and dance. A poet and internet comedian, he is known for his unique fusion of humor, musical dialectic, and artistic expression.

Adama Diouf

Born into N'Guewel Senegalese lineage, Adama Diouf was raised in West African traditions. From age ten, he began drumming in shows and healing ceremonies. Today, he brings that energy, power and rich heritage to every performance and teaching experience.

Sebastian "Sebi" Feo

Producer, event coordinator, and owner of Island Time Productions, Sebastian "Sebi" Feo bridges traditional and contemporary percussion with his own distinct style.

Hamun, the Human

Hamun is a sound alchemist and cultural diplomat raised in Tehran, now showcasing the beauty of Iran to people of all backgrounds. Through his collective project Sounds of Unity he explores the fusion and connection of our ancestry.

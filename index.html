About

Rooted in tradition.
Alive in Los Angeles.

We are The Mafé Ensemble — blending traditional West African rhythms with a fresh West Coast energy. Born in the historic Leimert Park neighborhood of Los Angeles, we bring an electric spirit of connection, community, and joy to every performance. Composed of multi-instrumental musicians, we create a diasporic fusion, combining ancient and modern rhythms.

Our work has taken us from opening the LA City Council Juneteenth Celebration to the 2026 Oscars, where founding members participated in the Sinners "I Lied to You" performance. Beyond the spotlight, we're committed to education — offering community classes, interactive performances, and youth workshops. Since 2022, we've partnered with LAUSD through Star Education K-12, developing curriculum and launching West African drum programs in schools across Los Angeles.

Members

The Ensemble

Saliou M'Boup

Saliou M'Boup is a multi-instrumentalist from Venice, California who has performed across all genres of music, specializing in West African drumming and teaching. Continuing his family legacy, his father is a Senegalese griot and master drummer who served as director of music for Ballet Bougarabou. Saliou brings deep roots and authentic tradition to every performance.

Since 2022, he has been working within LAUSD through Star Education K-12, creating curriculum and launching new West African Drum classes, with a focus on early childhood development. He also leads a 16-week rock and roll course where students perform at iconic LA venues like The Mint and The Troubadour. Along with other Mafé members, he works as a guest artist at various public and private schools throughout Los Angeles teaching social and emotional learning through percussion and rhythm.

Shannon Tobin

Shannon Tobin is a multi-instrumentalist who moves effortlessly between genres. A Los Angeles native with deep roots in jazz composition, he brings rare versatility to The Mafé Ensemble. Whether he's laying down bass lines or blending traditions into something entirely new, his presence shapes every performance. His credits include the US National Tour with Red Elvis's (2023–2025), playing bass and balalaika. Currently completing his Music Industry Studies degree at CSUN, bridging performance and professional artistry.
